Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pandas ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application Pandas (the "App"), a puzzle game where you guide a group of pandas to safety. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

Solo play works entirely on your device and sends no data anywhere. The App's optional 2-player co-op mode uses Firebase (a Google service) and collects:

  • Anonymous account ID — created automatically on first launch via Firebase Authentication. It is a random identifier, not tied to your name, email address, or any other personal information. We never ask for those.
  • Game session data — when you create or join a co-op room, gameplay data (a short room code, panda positions, terrain changes, and skill commands) is synced between the two players' devices via Firebase Firestore. This describes the state of the game, not the players.

2. How We Use Your Information

Game session data is used solely to synchronize a co-op match between two players in real time. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

3. Data Storage

Co-op session data is stored in Firebase Firestore, a Google Cloud service. We do not maintain any other servers or databases.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses Firebase (Google) for anonymous authentication and co-op sync. It does not integrate any advertising SDKs or third-party analytics.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
