Common questions

  • The app won't sync between two devices. Both players need an internet connection for Private (by code) and Random Match modes. Solo mode works fully offline.
  • I lost my level progress. Progress is stored locally on your device and isn't tied to an account, so it can't be restored after reinstalling or switching devices.
