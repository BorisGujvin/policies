Autosite Bridge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Autosite Bridge ("the app") lets a website owner receive editing requests over WhatsApp and have them applied to their site automatically. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights.

1. WhatsApp Messages

When you message the connected WhatsApp Business number, the message text and your phone number are received via the WhatsApp Cloud API and temporarily stored in a MySQL database on the site owner's web hosting, so they can be picked up and processed.

Messages are used solely to carry out the requested website change. They are not used for marketing, profiling, or shared with any party other than as described in this policy.

2. AI Processing

To act on your request, the message text is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service (Claude Code), which interprets the request and makes the corresponding change to the website's code. The AI request includes the message text and, where relevant, recent conversation history for context.

3. Data Retention

Message records are marked as processed once picked up and are not retained for longer than needed for delivery and short-term conversation context. They are not used to build a long-term profile of you.

4. Access Control

Only phone numbers explicitly allow-listed by the website owner can trigger website changes through this app. Messages from other numbers are ignored.

5. Third Parties

Data passes through two third-party services in the course of normal operation: Meta (WhatsApp Cloud API), which delivers your message to us, and Anthropic (Claude), which processes the request text. Their respective privacy policies apply to their handling of data:

  • WhatsApp Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic Privacy Policy

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL.
